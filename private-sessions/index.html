Begin where you are.

Our free 20-minute intro call is where we begin. We’ll talk about what you’re curious about, how you’re feeling about movement, and what you’d like to explore. I’ll use that conversation to shape the invitations I offer in your private session, with your interests, comfort, and pace in mind.

Somatic means relating to the body. Rather than planning what a movement should look like, we bring attention to how it feels: the contact of your feet with the floor, a breath, a stretch, a rhythm, or the impulse to pause. You don’t need to know what you’re feeling straight away. Numbness, uncertainty, and “I don’t know” are welcome starting points too.

Through music, guided cues, and free-form movement, we make space to feel, express, play, and explore a sense of grounding. If something feels stuck, you might experiment with a different rhythm, a gesture, a sound, or stillness — following what feels available rather than trying to force a release. Your body’s sensations and your own choices guide the experience.

It’s not about how it looks. It’s about how it feels. Beautiful shapes can be part of dance, but here you’re invited to move without judging your movement from the outside. Embodiment is an invitation to be present with your body and notice what it’s asking for.

A session may include gentle movement exploration, music, noticing sensations, or rest. You can adapt, slow down, observe, or stop at any time. No particular emotional result is promised; this is not psychotherapy or medical treatment.

How it works

  1. Start with a free 20-minute video call. Meet Sophie, share what you’re curious about, and ask anything.
  2. Complete the participant agreement before the call. Sophie will send the current agreement for review and signature. If it isn’t ready, the call will need to wait.
  3. See how it feels. Talk about your interests, questions, and whether working together feels like a fit. There’s no pressure to continue.
  4. Arrange your first session. If you’d like to continue, Sophie will arrange payment and scheduling directly with you.
  5. Pay in advance, then receive confirmation. Your paid session is confirmed only after Sophie verifies payment and the appointment details.

Stillness can start with movement.

Find it hard to sit still and meditate? You might prefer to begin by moving. Dance has long been part of how people gather, express themselves, and mark meaningful moments. Here, movement can be a way to explore your senses before choosing a quieter pause — without needing to arrive at a particular state of calm.

Make a little room.

Sessions take place online. Wear something you can move in, find a clear space that feels comfortable, and have water nearby. Seated and smaller movements are welcome. Cameras are encouraged so we can share an interactive experience and Sophie can respond to your movement. Discuss any camera or access needs with Sophie before booking.
