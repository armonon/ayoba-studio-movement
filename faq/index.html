A few things you might be wondering

Curiosity is
a good beginning.

No question is too small. Here’s a little clarity before you step in.

What is somatic movement?

Somatic means relating to the body. In this practice, your body is the starting point: instead of thinking through how to move or what a shape should look like, you tune into sensations and let movement emerge from there. Guided invitations draw attention to breath, contact, rhythm, and what feels available. It’s okay to feel unsure, numb, or not know what you’re noticing. There are no steps to get right. It’s not about how it looks; it’s about how it feels.

What is ecstatic dance?

Ecstatic dance is a free-form movement practice with music and room for individual expression. Different communities approach it in different ways. At Ayoba, it’s a space to explore beyond familiar movement habits, play with energy and expression, and connect with others while following how your own body wants to move. Somatic invitations bring attention to sensation; free-form dance offers room to explore it. You don’t need to reach bliss, have a release, or produce any particular experience.

Do I need dance experience?

No. Complete beginners, experienced dancers, and everyone in between are welcome. You don’t need steps or a sense of rhythm.

What if I feel awkward or self-conscious?

That is a valid place to begin. Try something small, stay seated, pause, or observe. You’re welcome to voice anything you want during the session, and we can explore what would help you feel more comfortable. You do not need to become confident before taking part, and you can choose how much to explore. It’s not about how it looks. It’s about how it FEELS.

What should I wear?

Anything comfortable that lets you move, sit, and rest. Choose footwear or bare feet according to your own space, balance, and comfort. No special dance clothing is needed.

How much space do I need?

Enough clear space for the movements you choose, away from obstacles and hazards. You can keep movement small or seated. Discuss your space and access needs with Sophie before booking.

Can I sit out or stop whenever I want?

Yes. Participation is voluntary. You can sit, observe, adapt, slow down, or stop any activity at any time. There is no need to push through discomfort.

What if emotions come up?

Movement and music can make room for feelings. You might notice tears, laughter, a wish to make a sound, or a need for quiet; you may also notice very little. None of these responses has to be encouraged, stopped, or interpreted as proof of healing. You’re welcome to express what feels comfortable, pause, rest, or stop. You don’t need to retell past experiences or push toward an emotional release. Tell Sophie what you need if you wish. The practice is not psychotherapy or medical treatment; professional support may be more appropriate for feelings that are overwhelming.

Do I have to keep my camera on?

Keeping your camera on is encouraged. It helps make the session interactive: Sophie can see your movement, check in with you, and adapt her guidance as you explore together. If being on camera feels difficult or you have an access or privacy need, speak with Sophie before the session so you can agree on an approach. A camera does not replace your own choices about comfort and safety.

Are these sessions therapy?

No. Ayoba Studio offers creative somatic movement and ecstatic dance experiences. These are not psychotherapy, medical treatment, or a substitute for professional mental-health or medical care. No particular emotional or physical outcome is promised.

How do I book a free intro call?

Use the intro-call link to contact Sophie. The online calendar is awaiting configuration, so an inquiry does not reserve a time. Sophie must confirm the date, time zone, and video-call details directly. The current participant agreement must be completed before the call.

What happens after the intro call?

If you would like to continue, Sophie will discuss a private session, arrange payment, and agree on scheduling. There is no obligation to book a paid session.

Sophie will send verified PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle instructions. Payment is made in advance and checked by Sophie before the paid session is confirmed.

Choose an amount from $15 to $35 USD for the same 60-minute online group class. Confirm your chosen amount with Sophie before paying. If Sophie cancels, you receive a full refund or may choose to reschedule.

How does the free introductory group class work?

One introductory group class is available for each eligible new participant. Contact Sophie to check eligibility and available places. No classes are currently open for registration.

What happens if a group class doesn’t meet minimum enrollment?

Sophie checks registered enrollment exactly 48 hours before the scheduled class. Fewer than five registered participants means cancellation. Everyone registered is emailed, all collected class payments are refunded in full, and unpaid registrants are not charged. These steps are manual at launch.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

For all paid private sessions and group classes, cancel more than 24 hours before the start for a full refund. Participant cancellations 24 hours or less before the start are nonrefundable. If Sophie cancels, including when a group class doesn’t meet its minimum enrollment, you receive a full refund or may choose to reschedule. Free intro calls can be canceled or rescheduled without charge. Read the full policy.

What happens if my internet connection fails?

Pause safely and try to reconnect if you wish. Contact Sophie as soon as you can. A separate connectivity policy has not been set, so discuss any relevant arrangements before paying. The standard participant-cancellation policy applies to all paid sessions and classes; an automatic refund or reschedule for connection problems is not promised.

How is my information handled?

This site uses no analytics, mailing-list service, or embedded calendar. The contact form sends your name, email, topic, and message to Netlify Forms, where submissions are stored in the site’s private Netlify dashboard. An email notification is configured for Sophie. Sending a message does not create a booking. Email and hosting providers handle information under their own policies. See the Privacy Policy for the current setup. Read the privacy policy.
