The practical things

Plans change.
Here’s the policy.

Clear expectations, before you commit to payment.

Free introductory calls

Free 20-minute intro calls may be canceled or rescheduled without charge. Contact Sophie to arrange a change.

Paid private sessions and group classes

  • More than 24 hours before the appointment: a participant cancellation of any paid private session or group class receives a full refund.
  • 24 hours or less before the appointment: participant cancellations of any paid private session or group class are nonrefundable.
  • If Sophie cancels: receive a full refund or choose to reschedule.

The one-time $45 introductory paid session is a paid private session and follows the same terms. Regular sessions are $65 for 45 minutes. All prices are in USD.

Group journeys

A class needs a minimum of five registered participants. Enrollment is checked exactly 48 hours before the scheduled class.

  • If the minimum is not reached, the class is canceled and all payments collected for that class are refunded in full. Unpaid registrants are not charged.
  • If Sophie cancels for another reason, participants receive a full refund or may choose to reschedule.

The same participant-cancellation window applies to group classes: more than 24 hours before the start receives a full refund; 24 hours or less before the start is nonrefundable. Organizer cancellations and cancellations for insufficient enrollment remain fully refundable. No additional nonrefundable fees are imposed by this policy.

Payments and refund handling

Payments are made in advance through a verified payment method agreed with Sophie. Initial methods are PayPal, Venmo, and Zelle; no payment account or checkout is connected to this website.

Payments and refunds are manually verified and processed. A payment request, clicked link, email, or screenshot alone does not mark payment received. Sophie verifies the actual payment record and tracks outstanding refunds until completed.

Connection difficulties

Pause safely if your connection fails, try reconnecting if you wish, and contact Sophie. A separate connectivity policy has not been set. Agree on relevant arrangements before paying; this page does not promise an automatic reschedule or refund for participant connection problems. The standard cancellation window applies to all paid private sessions and group classes.

Requesting a change or refund

Email Sophie.roze@proton.me ↗ with the session and change you are requesting. Include the appointment date, time, and time zone so the cancellation window can be checked accurately.

Founder review pending before public launch. The booking pages and manual email templates use these same terms.
