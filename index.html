03 — A small invitation, right now

What are you
feeling today?

Start wherever you are. Choose a feeling and try a tiny moment of movement, if you’d like.

You don’t have to change how you feel.
Just notice where you’re beginning.

An invitation, not a prescription. Adapt, pause, or do nothing at all.

Let’s put this one down

“But I don’t
know how
to dance.”

a perfectly good place to start

You don’t need steps, rhythm, special clothes, or a more confident version of yourself. It’s not about how it looks. It’s about how it feels.

You can move, pause, sit, observe, or adapt any invitation. Small counts. Stillness counts. Showing up as yourself counts.

Hi, I’m Sophie.

I’m a certified Somatic Movement & Ecstatic Dance Facilitator, recording artist, and curious human.

Dance, music, art, and nature keep bringing me back to a simple question: what does it feel like to be here, in this body, right now?

I created Ayoba Studio as a place to explore that question together.
