01 / Practice

Movement as play, as a tool, as a practice, as a meditation, as a way to connect to your body and embrace the motions of life.

The intention for class can be whatever you make it. You can simply just want to experience it. Just show up as you are.

If you want to just play, let’s play. If you wish to move with stuck energy and emotions, with the intention to release and let go, let’s do it.

If you want to get more grounded in your body and embrace the energy of embodiment, play, love… let’s do it all.
