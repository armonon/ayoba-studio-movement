02 / Group journeys · Online

Move together.
Be yourself.

Shared music. Individual expression. A little more play, a little less pressure, and room for human connection.

Group journeys combine music, guided movement invitations, and space to follow your own impulses. You might explore a gesture, let a rhythm grow, pause, or find a completely different way in.

It’s about being in community, too: sharing a space, bouncing off one another’s energy, and still staying with how your own body is asking you to move. You can be inspired by the people around you without needing to copy them or keep up.

You don’t need dance experience and you don’t have to perform for anyone. Beginners and experienced movers are welcome. Moving together doesn’t mean moving alike.
