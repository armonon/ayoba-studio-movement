The human behind the invitations

dance as art.

Hi, I’m Sophie.

Movement has been a thread through my life — starting with classes throughout my entire life, recitals, and eventually leading into free-form movement, the ecstatic dance community, and my own daily practice.

I’m now a certified Somatic Movement & Ecstatic Dance Facilitator. I’ve been attending Ecstatic Dance Los Angeles since I was 19 and spent a year living on Hawaiʻi’s Big Island, where I also took part in the island’s Ecstatic Dance community. That experience inspired me to study hula with an encouraging Hawaiian woman.

I’ve trained in jazz, contemporary, folk, ballet, tap, hip-hop, and modern, but free-form dance and dance as a tool for personal healing and expression have continued to stand out. I’ve also performed with movement groups at art galas. Across those different spaces, I keep coming back to the magic I find in movement — for healing, expression, and art.

A name with a personal thread.

My father was from South Africa, and I grew up visiting often. Learning dances from locals around town and encountering different cultural expressions of movement shaped my relationship with dance. These experiences humbled me and encouraged me to keep learning. Seeing how embedded dance was in the communities I encountered, and how it brought people together, made me think about its importance in human life: as a craft, a way of living, a gathering, and a form of ritual and renewal. Dance is culture.

Ayoba is a South African slang expression of excitement, approval, and enthusiasm — a little like “awesome!” It connects a personal part of my story with the joy and expression I hope to make room for here. My experiences are my own, and I’m not claiming authority on South African dance traditions by using this name.

More than one way to be creative.

I’m also a recording artist with released music, a video editor, and an on-camera performer. I have an Associate degree in Environmental Studies, and an ongoing interest in art, music, herbalism, nature, embodiment, creativity, and community.

Ayoba Studio brings those interests into a welcoming space for exploration. You don’t have to explain yourself perfectly or arrive knowing what you need. We can start with curiosity.
