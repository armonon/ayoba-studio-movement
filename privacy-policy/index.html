A little care
with your information.

This policy describes the site as currently built. Founder review is pending before public launch.

Who to contact

Ayoba Studio is founded by Sophie. For privacy questions or requests, email Sophie.roze@proton.me ↗.

What the website does today

The site serves pages, locally hosted fonts, and illustrative media. No analytics, advertising pixels, newsletter signup, payment checkout, embedded calendar, or e-signature service has been configured.

The movement playground works in your browser and does not ask for personal information. The application does not intentionally store your feeling selections or form entries in browser storage. Netlify Forms processes submitted messages and applies its own hosting and anti-spam controls.

Contact form and email

The contact form asks for your name, email address, topic, and message. When you select Send message, those details are submitted to Netlify Forms and stored in the site’s private Netlify dashboard, accessible to authorized site administrators. Netlify may also process request and anti-spam information, such as an IP address. An email notification is configured for Sophie’s Proton address so she can respond. A successful form submission confirms acceptance by the form service, not delivery to a particular inbox. You may instead use the direct email link.

If you email Sophie directly, your email provider and Sophie’s provider, Proton, process it. Form notifications are also processed by Netlify and Proton. Sophie uses inquiries to respond and arrange next steps. Do not include medical histories, diagnoses, or sensitive personal details in an ordinary inquiry. Ordinary email should not be treated as a secure clinical intake service.

Hosting and access

The site is hosted using Netlify. The hosting and access platform may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and authentication information according to its own policies. The site owner has not configured or independently verified platform log retention. This Netlify publication is publicly accessible.

Locally hosted fonts and images avoid contacting a third-party font or media service when viewing this site. External services are contacted only if you follow their links or open your email application.

Bookings, agreements, and payments

The site has no automatically connected calendar, payment processor, or e-signature API. A protected founder workspace supports manually maintained participant, session, agreement, payment, class, and refund records. A contact inquiry creates a message record in Netlify Forms; it does not create an appointment, class registration, signature, or payment record.

When services are connected, this policy must be updated to identify the providers, information collected, purposes, retention, and available choices. The founder must restrict access to participant records and signed agreements; no such records are published here.

Your information and choices

Contact Sophie to ask about information you have sent, or to request a correction or deletion. A fixed retention period has not yet been set. Authorized site administrators manage form submissions in Netlify, and Sophie should regularly review stored inquiries and remove those no longer needed. Contact Sophie to request a correction or deletion; deletion from the form dashboard does not automatically delete copies in an email mailbox. Specific technical, deletion, or confidentiality guarantees are not promised by this page.

Private operating records

When you arrange sessions with Sophie, authorized founder-workspace users may record your name, email, appointment details, agreement version and verified-signature references, class registrations, and manually verified payment or refund references. These records are stored in Netlify Blobs and encrypted by the application. The founder workspace requires a private passcode and uses a secure sign-in cookie lasting up to eight hours. It does not store your bank credentials or process payments. Signature verification is recorded from the actual signing provider; entering a record does not create a legal signature.

Ask Sophie about access, corrections, deletion, and retention of these operating records. Backups exported by the founder must also be kept private. This workspace does not provide medical or clinical records.

Changes to this policy

The policy should be reviewed whenever the booking, messaging, analytics, payment, or signature setup changes. Last site-policy update: October 29, 2026.
